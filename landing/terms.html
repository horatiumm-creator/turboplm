5. Billing, cancellation and refunds

Pro is billed monthly in advance through Stripe, which handles the payment and stores the card. We never see your card details. Your plan changes when Stripe tells us the payment succeeded, not when the checkout page closes.

An administrator can cancel at any time from the billing page in the application, which opens Stripe's own portal. Cancelling stops future charges; the organisation stays on Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for, and then returns to Free.

We do not run automatic refunds. If something went wrong — a duplicate charge, a subscription you meant to cancel and did not, a month you could not use — email us and we will sort it out. 6. What happens when a plan ends

Nothing is deleted, hidden or locked. An organisation that had 4,000 parts on Pro and drops to Free keeps all 4,000 of them and can keep reading and editing them; it simply cannot create the 4,001st, or add a second member, until it is on Pro again. This is a deliberate decision, not an oversight — a customer whose card expired should not lose access to their engineering record, and holding data hostage is not a way to collect a debt.
