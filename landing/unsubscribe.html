Unsubscribe

Stop hearing from us

One click below and you are off the list. No form to fill in, no account to make, and no question about why you are leaving.

Unsubscribe by email

That opens a message to office@turboplm.com with the subject already filled in. Send it as it is — you do not need to write anything. If your device does not open a mail client, write to that address from the account you want removed and put unsubscribe in the subject.

What happens then

Your address goes on a suppression list and we stop. In practice that is same-day; the law allows ten business days and we are not going to use them. The list is kept precisely so that we can tell we have already been asked — which means the address is retained for that purpose and nothing else, and it is never mailed, sold or shared.

This link keeps working. An unsubscribe mechanism has to stay reachable long after the message that carried it — this one is a permanent page and a mailbox that is read, so it will still work whenever you get round to it.

If you would rather not write to us

Marking the message as spam in your mail client also works, and we would rather you did that than receive another one. It is a blunter signal — it teaches your provider to filter us rather than telling us to stop — so the link above is better for both of us, but either is a clear answer and we will treat it as one.

This is not your TurboPLM account

This page is only about email we sent you. If you have an account at saas.turboplm.com, unsubscribing here does not close it and does not stop the messages the product itself sends — invitations to an organisation, or notifications about work assigned to you. Those come from your account and are managed there. To delete the account and its data, write to office@turboplm.com and say so; the privacy notice explains what is held and what happens to it.

Who is writing to you

The operator of TurboPLM. Registered address available on request. We are contactable at office@turboplm.com for this or anything else.
